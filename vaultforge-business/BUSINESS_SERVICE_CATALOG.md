# Business Service Catalog

Date: 2026-05-09

## Purpose

This catalog turns the current business preset, style, and mod names into an
operator-facing service menu for VaultForge Business.

It is not a price sheet, publication plan, license promise, or approval to run
live client generation. Those remain Nath-facing launch gates. This file only
documents what the business lane can describe, assemble, and review from its
current wrapper names and prompt-bank usage.

## Current Positioning

VaultForge Business is strongest as a structured brand-asset workflow for
small businesses, creators, communities, apps, and symbolic visual systems.

The first service shape should stay narrow:

- logo or brand-mark directions
- app, community, or product icons
- social covers and tiles
- brand boards
- small client starter packs
- symbolic or game-adjacent asset packs when the brief fits

The service is not fully autonomous. The client-ready path still depends on
human curation, output review, selected exports, saved prompts, saved
manifests, and a delivery package.

## Recommended Starter Offers

These are safe internal offer shapes for further testing and packaging docs:

- Brand starter direction: `business-logo`, `brand-board`,
  `clean-corporate` or `modern-startup`, plus `trustworthy` and `premium`.
- App or community icon: `business-icon`, `vector-crisp`, plus
  `small-size-readable`, `high-contrast`, and `transparent-bg-ready`.
- Social identity tile: `business-cover` or `social-brand-tile`,
  `editorial-brand`, plus `bold` and `premium`.
- Symbolic emblem pack: `badge-emblem` or `brand-mark`, `vector-crisp` or
  `mono-mark`, plus `high-contrast` and `print-safe`.

Only the first two are backed by several current prompt notes. The other two
should be treated as catalog candidates until examples are generated and
reviewed.

## Gated Items

Do not use this catalog as approval for:

- pricing, paid launch, public publication, or sales copy
- license, trademark, or commercial-use guarantees
- live generation without explicit approval
- moving business names into shared engine registries
- promising exact typography, SVG conversion, ICO export, or print production
