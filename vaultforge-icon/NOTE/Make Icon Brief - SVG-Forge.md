## Context

SVG-Forge is a small Windows-friendly helper for converting raster icon, logo,
and glyph inputs into SVG outputs. In the icon lane it represents careful
translation: taking rough or generated visual material and producing a cleaner,
reviewable vector form.

The icon should feel practical and tool-like, not mystical or over-decorated.
It should signal conversion, tracing, and shape refinement.

## Concept Directions

1. Raster-to-vector bridge
   - A small pixel grid on the left resolving into a clean outlined shape on the
     right.
   - Best for making the tool's function immediately legible.

2. Traced path node
   - A simple icon silhouette with a few visible Bezier nodes or anchor points.
   - Best for emphasizing SVG/vector editing without looking like a generic
     image app.

3. Forge stamp
   - A compact mark that combines a simple anvil/stamp shape with a crisp vector
     path.
   - Best if the lane wants stronger VaultForge identity while staying
     restrained.

## Style Notes

- Prefer crisp geometry, high contrast, and readable silhouette.
- Avoid busy gradients, heavy fantasy styling, or painterly detail.
- Keep the mark recognizable at small sizes.
- Use two or three colors at most if color is needed.
- A monochrome version should still work.
